Natural and Useful Proofs

Trustworthy and Useful

  • We talk a lot about verification and trust
  • If it is raining, I should take my umbrella. It is raining. Explain why I should take my umbrella.
    • LLMs answer naturally

      Whenever it is raining I should take my umbrella.
      It is raining, so the current situation falls under that rule.
      Therefore, I should take my umbrella.

    • ATPs answer unnaturally

      Assume that I do not take my umbrella.
      From that assumption we are led to the conclusion that it is not raining.
      But this contradicts the given fact that it is raining.
      Therefore the assumption that I do not take my umbrella must be false.
      So I should take my umbrella.

      WTF?!!?
