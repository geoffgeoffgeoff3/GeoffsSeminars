The Taelja Effect

The Problem

  • Prove ((((true ⊕ false) ⊕ false) ⊕ true) ⊕ false) = false
  • In the TPTP MSC005-1.p

The Refutation

  • By E - Proof DAG
  • In LLM English The prover starts by assuming that ((((true ⊕ false) ⊕ false) ⊕ true) ⊕ false) has no truth value. It then works backwards through the nested expression, establishing successively that (((true ⊕ false) ⊕ false) ⊕ true) != false, then ((true ⊕ false) ⊕ false) != true, and finally (true ⊕ false) != true. However, the axioms specify that (true ⊕ false) = true. This contradiction refutes the original assumption, proving that ((((true ⊕ false) ⊕ false) ⊕ true) ⊕ false) = false.
